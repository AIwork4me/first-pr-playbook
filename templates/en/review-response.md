## Suggested reply format

> Thanks for the feedback.
> **Addressed in:** `<commit-sha>`
> **Change:** What changed and why.
> **Verified:** The exact test and its real observed result.
> **Limitations:** Anything not tested.

Never fabricate SHAs, test successes, or approval states; the contributor makes and checks each decision.
